Contact us

Email help@wafuelrefund.com. Tell us what you were doing and what you saw (a screenshot helps), and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days. In the app you can also use ☰ → Help & support → Email app support, which includes your app version.

Common questions

How much will I get back?

Washington's fuel tax on each gallon, minus the sales tax on the fuel and a small Coastal Protection fee. Recently that has been about 22–26¢ a gallon. The app shows the state's current per-gallon figures under Help & support.

What do I need to get started?

Just your fuel receipts. Scan each one with the app when you fuel up. To file a claim, you'll also need a Fuel Tax Refund account with Washington State.

What's a Fuel Tax Refund account?

It's the account the Washington State Department of Licensing pays your refunds through. It's free, and you add it in License eXpress, the state's website for renewing boat and vehicle tabs. Most boaters already use License eXpress but haven't added a refund account.

To set one up, follow the state's guide, Set up a new unlicensed refund account. The state reviews each application before you can file. You can also start from the app: go to Settings → WA refund account → Connect. If you don't have a refund account, it takes you to the application, already signed in. Once the state approves it, tap Connect again.

Which receipts qualify?

Each receipt must show the six details marked below, as the state's rules require. Most fuel dock and gas station receipts already do. The app checks each receipt you scan and warns you if something is missing.

  1. Seller's name and address. Fuel bought out of state (for example, in Oregon or British Columbia) doesn't qualify.
  2. Date. The month, day and year. You must claim the receipt within 13 months of this date.
  3. Fuel type. It must say gasoline. The app doesn't claim diesel.
  4. Gallons.
  5. Price per gallon.
  6. Total.

The state also requires:

  • A receipt for each purchase. A card statement, or a card slip that shows only the amount, isn't enough.
  • No handwritten changes. The state won't accept a receipt with altered, crossed-out or erased details. If a receipt is wrong, ask the seller for a corrected one.
  • If you lose a receipt, ask the seller for a copy marked "copy" or "duplicate".
  • The fuel must be for your boat, not a car, trailer or anything else used on the road.
  • Keep your original receipts for five years after you file, in case the state asks for them.

When can I file?

Once a month has ended, as long as the claim covers at least $20 of state fuel tax (about 36 gallons). Each receipt must be claimed within 13 months of its date. The app lists claims under Ready to file when they're ready.

I found an old receipt. Can I still add it?

Yes, if it's less than 13 months old and you haven't already claimed its month. The state accepts only one claim for each month, so if you've claimed it, call the Department of Licensing about amending that claim.

Who files the claim?

The app organizes your receipts and prepares the complete claim, with everything attached, in your License eXpress account. Because the claim is in your name, you review it and submit it. After that, the Department of Licensing handles the claim directly with you, including any questions, follow-up and your refund payment.

Preparing my claim didn't finish

Nothing was submitted, so it's safe to try again, and it won't cost you anything. If it keeps happening, send us a report from the app and we'll look into it.

Does it work for diesel, aircraft or farm fuel?

No. The app is only for gasoline used in boats. Other fuel tax refunds are separate claims, which you file in License eXpress yourself. Dyed diesel never qualifies.

Where are my receipts kept?

Only on your phone. To keep a copy, use Settings → Export receipt photos in the app.

Questions about a claim or your refund account

The Washington State Department of Licensing decides claims and runs License eXpress. For questions about a submitted claim, a payment or your refund account, contact its Unlicensed Refunds Unit: 360-664-1838 or UnlicensedRefunds@dol.wa.gov. WA Fuel Refund isn't affiliated with the Department of Licensing.
