Support

Contact us

Email help@wafuelrefund.com. Tell us what you were doing and what you saw (a screenshot helps), and we'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days. In the app you can also use ☰ → Help & support → Email app support, which includes your app version.

Common questions

How much will I get back?

Washington's fuel tax on each gallon, less sales tax on the fuel and DOL's Coastal Protection fee. Recently that's been about 22–26¢ a gallon. The app shows DOL's current per-gallon calculation under Help & support.

What do I need to get started?

Just your fuel receipts. To file, you also need a Fuel Tax Refund account in Washington's License eXpress. It's separate from your vehicle tabs, even though you sign in the same way. The app's WA refund account page connects an existing one or takes you to DOL's application; approval may take a week or longer.

When can I file?

Once a month is over, if the claim covers at least $20 of state fuel tax (about 36 gallons). DOL needs each claim within 13 months of the earliest receipt in it. The app shows claims under Ready to file when they're ready.

I found an old receipt. Can I still add it?

Yes, if it's less than 13 months old and its month hasn't been claimed yet. If you've already claimed that month, DOL won't take a second claim for it; call DOL about amending that claim.

Why doesn't the app submit the claim by itself?

The claim is filed in your name and DOL asks you to certify it's true. The app fills in everything and attaches your receipts, then stops at DOL's Refund Summary so you can check DOL's numbers, tick the certification and tap Submit.

Filing stalled or a screen looks different

DOL's site varies a little between accounts. If the app stops on a screen, it tells you what it needs; you can finish that screen yourself and tap Continue. If you're stuck, email us with a screenshot of the screen and the app's message.

Does it work for diesel, aircraft or farm fuel?

Not yet. The app handles gasoline used in boats. DOL's other refunds are separate claims you'd file in License eXpress yourself. Dyed diesel never qualifies.

Where are my receipts kept?

Only on your phone. To keep a copy, use Settings → Export receipt photos in the app.

Questions about a claim or your DOL account

DOL decides claims and runs License eXpress. For questions about a submitted claim, payment or your refund account, contact DOL's Unlicensed Refunds Unit: 360-664-1838 or UnlicensedRefunds@dol.wa.gov. WA Fuel Refund isn't affiliated with DOL.
