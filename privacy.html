WA Fuel Refund

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

WA Fuel Refund (the “App”) and www.wafuelrefund.com (the “Website,” and together with the App, the “Services”) are provided by Orca Labs LLC (“Orca Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy describes the information we collect and process when you use the Services, how we use and disclose it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Information stored on your device. WA Fuel Refund does not have user accounts or a registration system. Fuel receipts, extracted purchase details, vessel and claimant information, supporting documents, and claim history are stored locally on your device. We do not store copies of these records on our servers. Information stored on your device may be included in backups managed by you or your backup provider.

Usage and diagnostic information. We collect limited information about how the Services are used and perform, such as general feature activity, approximate receipt and refund totals, filing-process outcomes, device or application characteristics, and error information. This information may be associated with a randomly assigned installation or similar identifier, allowing us to distinguish activity from one installation over time without knowing the user's identity. We do not include receipt images, government account numbers, claim confirmation numbers, vessel registrations, government sign-in credentials, or banking information in our routine usage metrics.

Purchase information. When you purchase claim preparation services, we may receive and retain limited transaction information, including the purchase identifier, amount, date, and status, to verify purchases, provide support, handle refund requests, and maintain business records. We do not receive your payment card information.

Information you provide to us. If you contact us, request early access, or choose to submit a diagnostic or problem report, we collect the contact details and other information you provide. A problem report may include information you voluntarily choose to share; please avoid including sensitive information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Website information. When you visit the Website, we and our service providers may collect standard technical and usage information, such as browser characteristics, pages visited, referral sources, and approximate location derived from network information. The Website may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and operation.

2. Access to DOL Services

WA Fuel Refund allows you to access your Washington State Department of Licensing (DOL) account to prepare refund claims. If you choose to save your login information for convenience, it is managed by your device's operating system using its built-in secure credential storage. Orca Labs does not collect, transmit to its servers, or retain copies of your login information.

3. Information Provided to DOL

When you use the claim preparation feature, the App transfers information and supporting materials necessary to prepare your application directly to DOL's License Express system. These materials may include vessel and claimant details, fuel purchase information, receipt copies, and supporting documents you choose to provide. You can review the prepared submission in the App before initiating this process.

The App assists in completing and saving the application in DOL's system as a draft. You are responsible for reviewing and certifying the claim and formally submitting it to DOL. Orca Labs does not receive or retain copies of the claim materials sent to DOL in the ordinary course of providing this feature. Information received by DOL is subject to its own practices and applicable law.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to operate and improve the Services; understand general usage; identify, investigate, and correct problems; verify purchases; respond to support requests; communicate about early access when requested; protect the Services against misuse; and comply with applicable legal and business obligations.

5. Payments and Service Providers

Preparation-fee payments in the App are processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play, as applicable, under the payment provider's terms and privacy practices. We do not process or store payment card details.

We use service providers to support hosting, analytics, diagnostics, purchase verification, and other operations. Such providers may process information on our behalf as reasonably necessary to provide those services or as required by law. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect our rights, users, or Services.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or disclose it to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

6. Retention and Security

Information stored in the App generally remains on your device until you remove it or delete the App, subject to your device's backup settings. We retain information received by us only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including support, troubleshooting, accounting, compliance, and dispute resolution. Retention periods may differ by category of information and applicable legal requirements.

We apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to information under our control. Information stored on your device also depends on the security of your device and its operating system. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can manage or delete receipts and other locally stored records using the App's available controls, and you can remove locally stored App information by deleting the App, subject to any device backups. You can choose whether to save your DOL credentials and whether to provide optional contact information or problem reports. Website analytics preferences, where available, can be managed through the Website or your browser settings.

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping obligations. Because the App does not require an account and routine usage metrics may use pseudonymous installation identifiers, we may need information from you to locate relevant records and may not always be able to associate a particular record with an individual. Deleting the App does not automatically delete information already received by us or information previously submitted to DOL.

8. Children's Privacy

The Services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

The Services may contain links to websites or features operated by third parties. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies. This includes DOL's License Express system and other third-party services you choose to access.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our Services or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version with an updated effective date and provide additional notice when required by law or when changes are material.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or requests regarding information held by Orca Labs LLC, contact us at help@wafuelrefund.com.
