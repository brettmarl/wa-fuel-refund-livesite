WA Fuel Refund

Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains how the WA Fuel Refund app and the website www.wafuelrefund.com (together, the "Service") handle information. The Service is provided by Orca Labs LLC ("Orca Labs", "we", "us").

The short version

  • The app doesn't ask you to create an account. Receipt contents and personal details stay on your phone.
  • We use Firebase to collect installation activity and rounded receipt, gallon and refund totals, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • When you file, the app sends your claim to Washington's Department of Licensing (DOL), the same as filing by hand.
  • The website uses Google Analytics for visits, traffic sources and download-link taps. We don't show ads or sell your information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in its private storage on your phone; only the rounded summary totals described below are included in usage metrics:

  • Receipt photos you take or choose, and what's read from them (date, total, gallons, fuel type).
  • Your claims: which receipts were claimed, when, and DOL's confirmation number and refund amount.
  • Details you enter: boat registration number, name and phone number, used to fill in your claims.
  • Which License eXpress account you connected (its account number and name as DOL shows them), and the last four digits of the bank account DOL shows for direct deposit, if any.

If you choose to save your License eXpress sign-in, your username and password are stored in your iPhone's Keychain, on that device only, and are filled in on DOL's sign-in page after Face ID or your passcode. They aren't sent anywhere else.

We don't receive these receipt contents or personal details through usage metrics. It may be included in your phone's own backups (for example iCloud Backup) if you have them turned on; those are governed by your backup provider.

Information sent to others

  • Washington State Department of Licensing. The app downloads DOL's published fuel tax rates from dol.wa.gov. When you file a claim, connect a refund account or set one up, the app opens DOL's License eXpress website inside the app and, for claims, fills in the claim and attaches your receipt photos and a spreadsheet of your receipts. That information goes to DOL, as it would if you entered it yourself, and DOL's own privacy policy and Washington public records laws apply to it.
  • Apple and Google. If you download the app from the App Store or Google Play, they may collect information under their own privacy policies (for example download counts and crash reports you agree to share with developers).
  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and use it only to reply and help you.
  • Problem reports. If filing stops on something unexpected, you can choose to send us a report by email. It contains the steps the app took and a copy of the DOL page where it stopped, with numbers and typed-in values masked, though your name or address may still appear. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it, and your password is never included.

App usage metrics

We use Google Firebase to store a randomly assigned installation identifier, first and last activity times, app version and platform, receipt count, whole-gallon totals, whole-dollar unclaimed estimates and submitted refund totals, and the number of submitted claims. We also record app opens, receipt saves, and setup, connection and filing runs: progress signal names, numeric steps and results. These help us understand use and fix problems.

Metrics do not contain receipt photos or text, purchase dates or individual receipt amounts, names, phone numbers, boat registrations, DOL account IDs, sign-ins, bank details or DOL page contents. Firebase processes technical connection information to provide its service. Installation identifiers distinguish installations, not verified people; we do not try to match them to website visitors.

You can stop future uploads in Settings → Share usage metrics. Events may queue on the device while offline; the queue is limited to the most recent 500 events and is cleared when you turn sharing off. Metrics already received remain until deleted. To request deletion of received metrics, contact us before turning sharing off or deleting the app so we can help identify the installation.

Camera, photos and Face ID

The camera is used only to photograph receipts, and photos you pick from your library are used only as receipts. Text is read on your phone. Face ID is handled by iOS; the app never receives your face data.

The website

www.wafuelrefund.com is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Analytics 4 to measure visits, approximate unique browsers, traffic sources and taps on store and early-access links. Google Analytics uses browser identifiers and cookies. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our configuration. GitHub and Google may process technical connection information under their own privacy policies. (applies to this browser). Disabling analytics stops future measurement; existing cookies may remain until cleared in your browser.

How long information is kept

Information on your phone is kept until you delete it in the app or delete the app. We review received app metrics after six months and delete them when no longer needed to understand usage or resolve problems. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to help you and then deleted within a reasonable time.

Security

Receipt contents and personal details stay on your device, protected by your phone's own security. Saved sign-ins use the iOS Keychain, limited to this device and only available when it's unlocked. Connections to DOL and Firebase use HTTPS. Received app metrics are restricted to the developer's authorized admin account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your phone locked.

Your choices and rights

You can view, edit or delete any receipt in the app, export your receipt photos from Settings, and delete the app to remove everything it stores. You can turn off future usage metrics in Settings and ask us to delete metrics or emails we hold. Depending on where you live, you may have other privacy rights; contact us to exercise them.

Children

The Service is meant for adults who own and operate boats. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Not affiliated with DOL

WA Fuel Refund and Orca Labs LLC aren't affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for the Washington State Department of Licensing.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above, and for significant changes we'll also say so in the app before the change takes effect.
